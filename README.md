# Publisher Monetization — Media.net Product Case Study

### Understanding Media.net's publishers: where they get stuck, and what could help.

**Product Management Case Study | Independent Student Project**

This case study explores the publisher monetization journey on **Media.net**, with a focus on understanding where small publishers may face friction when trying to understand and resolve ad-monetization problems.

The project uses publicly available publisher reports and competitor documentation to generate and test a product hypothesis.

---

## The Product Question

> **What friction do small publishers face when they try to understand and resolve ad-monetization problems?**

Rather than assuming a specific churn driver, I mapped the publisher journey and used public evidence to identify areas worth investigating.

---

## Approach

**Publisher Journey → Public Evidence → Hypothesis → Product Direction → Validation**

* Mapped the publisher journey from application through payment and support
* Reviewed and verified publicly available publisher reports
* Coded recurring themes across support, reporting, payments, approval and other areas
* Compared the findings with Google AdSense's publisher-facing resolution flows
* Developed a working product hypothesis
* Defined a validation plan before proposing a full product solution

---

## Key Finding

The evidence was **mixed, limited and not representative**, so the project does not claim that a particular problem is widespread among Media.net publishers.

However, several severe cases involved publishers reporting difficulty understanding what had happened and/or getting a satisfactory response.

This led to the following **working hypothesis**:

> Small publishers may benefit from clearer visibility into issue status, ownership, next steps and resolution paths when monetization problems occur.

This remains a hypothesis to validate.

---

## Product Direction

A proposed **issue-resolution layer** within the publisher dashboard.

The concept would help publishers understand:

* What is happening
* What is confirmed vs. unknown
* Who owns the next step
* What action the publisher needs to take
* Where to get help
* What happens next

The concept is illustrative and is **not presented as Media.net's existing workflow**.

---

## Validation Plan

Before building the proposed solution:

1. Conduct 5 conversations with small publishers/site owners
2. Test whether issue-resolution friction is a recurring problem
3. Identify which issue type matters most
4. Prototype one scenario
5. Conduct usability testing
6. Reconsider the product direction if the evidence does not support the hypothesis

---

## Evidence Limitations

The research used 12 verified public reports:

* 9 from 2017–2020
* 3 from 2022–2023
* 0 from 2024–2026

The evidence is directional rather than representative. The project therefore distinguishes between **what is observed, what is hypothesized, and what still needs to be tested**.
